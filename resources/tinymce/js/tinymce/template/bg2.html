About Diyiye
Xiamen First Page Network Technology Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "First Page") was formally established in 2006 and germinated in 2004. After 18 years of steady development, First Page has now become a strong integrated foreign trade promotion service company with a core technical team of more than 100 people. The company's technical headquarters is located in Hefei, a national-level science and technology and cultural city, and the sales headquarters is located in Xiamen, a garden city on the sea. At the same time, there are branches in Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Fuzhou, Nanjing, Quanzhou, Zhongshan, Wuxi and other places.
In February 2015, First Page officially became the core partner of Google China, the world's largest search engine. In January 2016, the People's Government of Huli District, Xiamen, Google Advertising (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., and Xiamen First Page Network Technology Co., Ltd. jointly promoted the official landing of the Xiamen Google Overseas Experience Center, the first in Fujian Province and the sixth in the country, in Xiamen Huli High-tech Park. First Page is responsible for project operations.
As the first foreign trade integrated marketing service provider in China that focuses on using Google as the traffic center and marketing-oriented self-built websites as the carrier, the first page has been following Google's purpose for 18 years, constantly analyzing and studying search engine optimization rules, and has provided foreign trade integrated marketing and promotion services to tens of thousands of small and medium-sized enterprises across the country. From marketing website construction to high-quality inquiry conversion, the first page uses the latest and most efficient methods and technologies to help more customers go further and further on the road of overseas promotion!
